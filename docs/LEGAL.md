# Legal and Compliance Notes

This document is informational and is not legal advice.

- A smart contract is not a legally recognized will in most jurisdictions. Pair it with a legal will that names an executor and references the on-chain mechanism.
- Inheritance, tax and data-protection rules differ by jurisdiction. Users should consult a qualified professional.
- The platform is non-custodial and collects minimal data, which is encrypted client-side.
- Any future production release should include a jurisdiction review before launch.
